How they are made

The rules are the product. They are also the reason each one looks like its own company instead of like a video tool.

  • Only your own material. Your screenshots, untouched. No mockups, no redrawn interface, no stock phone frames, no stock hands.
  • Your real brand values. Colours are read out of your own CSS, not eyedropped off a screenshot. Type is set from the actual font file your site loads, not a lookalike.
  • No invented numbers. The only figures on screen are ones already printed in your own material. I would rather leave a slot empty than make up a statistic about your product.
  • Your words where they beat mine. Four of the five close on the company's own headline rather than a tagline I wrote.
  • Built frame by frame in HTML and rendered deterministically — so a change is a change to one line, not a re-edit. That is what makes a 24-hour turnaround honest rather than a promise I have to hustle to keep.
